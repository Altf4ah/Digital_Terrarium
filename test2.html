Neural network

The brain

20 inputs (vision in 5 sectors for food, hazards and other creatures, plus energy, memory, comfort and pain), 14 hidden neurons and 5 outputs: turn, thrust, bite and two memory cells. 369 weights.

Reinforcement learning

Learning in a lifetime

Actions are noisy. Weights shift toward whatever preceded a reward (meals, kills) and away from pain (toxins, bites taken, bad climate). Traces carry credit back a few moves.

Self-supervised learning

Curiosity

A linear model predicts the next view of the world. Its error is surprise, and surprise pays a bonus scaled by the curiosity gene.

Genetics

13 genes

Vision, field of view, speed, body size, curiosity, learning rate, diet, aggression, sociality, preferred climate, breeding strategy, mutation rate itself, and lineage colour. Crossover only happens between compatible lineages, so species form.

Ecology

Planet rules

A drifting sun sets temperature, so creatures must adapt or migrate. Food grows best in warm fertile patches. Toxic pools drain energy. Radiation zones multiply mutation in babies born there.
