Neural network

The brain

A small feed-forward network with a recurrent memory loop reads the creature's senses and outputs how much to turn and how hard to move. It has 220 weights.

Reinforcement learning

Learning in a lifetime

The creature adds noise to its actions, then nudges its weights toward whatever preceded a reward. Meals pay, toxic pools cost. An eligibility trace spreads credit back over the last few moves.

Self-supervised learning

Curiosity

A linear model predicts the next sensory frame. Its prediction error is the creature's surprise, and surprise earns a small reward scaled by a curiosity gene.

Neuroevolution

Reproduction

A creature with enough energy has a child. Weights and traits are copied with mutation, or mixed with a nearby mate. Less efficient brains starve, so good ones spread.
